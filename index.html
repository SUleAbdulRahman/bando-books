About

Named after a village near Elmina.

Bando is a village near Elmina, where our founder grew up. The name is a reminder of where practical knowledge is needed most.

Bando Books is an independent publisher registered in Ghana. We publish work that helps people build things that last: businesses, teams, cooperatives and careers. Our first title, From Effort to Systems by Sule Abdul Rahman, comes from his work alongside farmers, cooperatives, agripreneurs and organisations in Northern Ghana.

Our approach to AI

Bando Books publishes practical knowledge drawn from real work. Our authors may use AI tools to help draft, edit or research, but every book we publish must rest on the author’s own experience, cases and judgement. Where AI has supported the writing, we say so in the book. Every author is responsible for checking all facts, figures and sources before publication, and every book is reviewed against that standard before it goes to print.

From From Effort to Systems, in the author’s acknowledgements:

AI writing tools supported the drafting and editing of this book. Responsibility for its content is mine alone: every experience, case and conclusion is my own, drawn from my work in Ghana; figures from other sources are cited where they appear. Any errors or omissions are mine. Sule Abdul Rahman
